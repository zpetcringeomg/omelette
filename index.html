this says more about you

4th Feb 2026

I want to be nicer to myself and let myself not be bothered by comments I know are very immature and just objectively not kind.

I am learning to tell myself that certain things: comments or actions directed towards me say a lot more about the person from whom they originated, than they do about me. And this makes life a tad bit easier, for me.

It eases the soul; but only if you really believe it- although that does not really make anything objectively true. But like anyways.

a random piece of my state of mind

What if I wanted all the happiness that I shouldn't have right now? What if I threw up all my emotions into all the wrong hands? I think too much about my life; that's probably not right, is it? Fucking over my future self- it's the one thing I can do real good. I could put my lilac slippers on; find my way back to where I came from, but is that what I really want, or am I just afraid of growing up. Every step weaves a part of a driven mess- and I'm not sure I can handle all of this. But as time passes, I want to be someone new, and love new things every single day.
