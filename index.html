this says more about you

4th Feb 2026

I want to be nicer to myself and let myself not be bothered by comments I know are very immature and just objectively not kind.

I am learning to tell myself that certain things: comments or actions directed towards me say a lot more about the person from whom they originated, than they do about me. And this makes life a tad bit easier, for me.

It eases the soul; but only if you really believe it- although that does not really make anything objectively true. But like anyways.
